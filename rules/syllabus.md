# MRCPsych theory syllabus — topic taxonomy

Source: RCPsych MRCPsych Examination Syllabus (applies to theory exams from January 2027),
https://www.rcpsych.ac.uk/training/exams/preparing-for-exams/mrcpsych-examination-syllabus

- `topic_id` is the syllabus subsection number (e.g. `3.3`). Exception: the three disorder
  buckets 6.1 (adults), 7.5 (older people) and 9.7 (children and adolescents) are split per
  disorder (e.g. `6.1.2` = mood disorders in adults). `6.1`, `7.5`, `9.7` themselves are NOT valid IDs.
- `paper` is fixed by section: sections 1–4 = Paper A, sections 5–13 = Paper B.
- `covers` lists the syllabus items under each topic, condensed. Use it to match questions.
- Only IDs in the tables below are valid. `scripts/validate.py` reads these tables.
